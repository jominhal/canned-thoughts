The hobby home of Canned Thoughts — written and hosted by Jominhal “Jomz” Pagapulaan.

this page is where i put the thoughts that arrive at the wrong hours.

the ones that show up at 3 am. in the elevator. in line for food. in the middle of a shift, when i should be thinking about work.

i used to just let them pass. most of them were gone by morning.

now i write them down before they go.

some are stories. some are confessions. some are one line i could not shake for a week.

nothing here is advice. i am not wise. i am just awake at the wrong times.

if one of them says the thing you could not say out loud — then it was never only mine to begin with.

Can № 1 · Word can

Goodbye

By Jominhal Pagapulaan · Canned Thoughts, October 20, 2026

I thought it had been a long time since I last prayed.

Then I realized I'd been praying every day. I just didn't know goodbye counted.

Centuries ago, people said it in full: God be with you. Said often enough, over long enough, a prayer wore down like a coin in a pocket. God be with you. Godbwye. Goodbye.

The word grew smaller until it could pass for ordinary. But the blessing never left it. It just learned to travel light.

Spanish never hid it. Adiós. To God.

Every farewell, the same quiet surrender: I can only walk with you this far. May something greater than me take you the rest of the way.

And I say it constantly.

Every ingat ka. Every chat me when you get home. Every fistbump, every door I've watched close behind someone I love.

I thought those were habits. Things we said because we cared, little rituals we performed without thinking.

But maybe I've been asking for the same thing all along.

That you arrive safely. That life is kind to you. That wherever you go, whatever happens when I'm no longer there to see it, may you be okay.

I thought prayer required folded hands, a bowed head, a moment set apart from the noise of living.

Maybe mine was always an open hand, waving.

Maybe every time I let someone go with an ingat ka, I was placing them in the care of something I could not be.

And maybe that's what goodbye has always been: the moment love reaches the limit of its own presence, and hopes it isn't the limit of its protection.

So if I've ever said goodbye to you, I've prayed for you.

I just didn't know it at the time.

I know it now.

God be with you.

Even when I can't be.

Godbwye.

Can № 2 · Word can

Esmiringhoy

By Jominhal Pagapulaan · Canned Thoughts, October 21, 2026

I laughed when I first heard it.

Esmiringhoy.

A wife said it on national television. About her husband. About a thing he kept refusing. The host stopped and asked what it meant.

Everyone laughed. Then everyone understood.

I kept thinking about the word itself. Not what it means. Why it had to exist.

Say it slowly. Es-mi-ring-hoy.

It sounds like a snack. A street game. Something a kid would name a stray dog. It doesn't sound like what it means at all.

Maybe that's the point.

Some things are too heavy to say plainly. So we make them smaller. We dress them in a joke. We let the funny word carry the sentence we can't.

Every house runs on words like these. The real sentence is never said. But everybody knows it. Everybody hears it anyway.

I don't think that's dishonesty. I think it's care. Some things can only be said softly, or they break something on the way out.

She couldn't say it at home. So she said it in front of the whole country. And even then, she brought the smaller word.

It worked anyway.

We all have one of those words.

Mine would sound just as stupid.

Maybe that's how you know it's doing its job.
